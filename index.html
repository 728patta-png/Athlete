Tiger Woods

American professional golfer widely considered one of the greatest in history.

82 PGA Tour wins and 15 major championships; he is the youngest to complete the career Grand Slam.

Age: 50 years
Net worth: 1.49 billion USD

He won three PGA Tour events and the 1997 Masters in his rookie year, setting a record by winning the Masters by 12 strokes.

He became the first African American golfer to win the Masters.
